## Recording

The deck has 13 widescreen slides. The Word script gives each slide its own page, with a silent delivery cue, target duration and cumulative finish time. PowerPoint Presenter View contains the same cues and narration. The target durations sum to 14:00.

| Speaking pace | Time for 1,751 spoken words |
|---|---:|

These estimates exclude pauses. At 130 words per minute, the 14-minute target allows about 32 seconds for transitions and pointing. Counts use whitespace-separated spoken words, including written-out numbers. Headings, timing lines, cues and references are excluded.

Rehearse slides 6–7 together so the change of reference is clear before the bars change order. On slide 9, compare the lines vertically at each linking setting. On slides 11–12, pause on the exploratory qualification and distinguish the three scopes of cost.
